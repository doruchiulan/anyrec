## A clearer recording panel

- The menu bar's recording panel is now one line (red dot, clock, title and app, and a small level bar per track) above a full-width **Stop Recording** button.
- The level bars no longer show yellow for normal speech. They are green when healthy, orange only near clipping, and grey when quiet or muted.
- **Muted in Teams** (or Slack) shows while the call can't hear your microphone.
- Quit is no longer offered next to Stop while recording; Open AnyRec is.
