## Recordings have a new home

- Recordings move from `~/Desktop/AnyRec Recordings` to `~/Library/Application Support/anyrec/Recordings`, each in a folder named after the call, like "2026-11-18 15.29 QA & Doru - Blueprint – Slack".
- Inside each folder: the video, `Transcript.md` and `Summary.md` at the top, with the raw tracks, data and cache in their own subfolders.
- Existing recordings are moved automatically when the app starts. Nothing is deleted or overwritten, and an interrupted move resumes on the next launch.
- **Settings › Recording** shows where recordings are kept and can move them to another folder.

## Share recordings with their notes

- **Share** prepares the video with the transcript as captions (speakers named) and the title, date and summary in its details, alongside `Summary.md` and `Transcript.md`. Your recording itself is not changed.
- The share menu offers **Save to OneDrive** and **Save to iCloud Drive** for every synced folder on your Mac.
