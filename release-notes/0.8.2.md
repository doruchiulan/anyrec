## Much smaller recordings

- The screen is recorded in HEVC, about a third smaller than before at the same quality.
- Once a recording is saved, AnyRec compresses its video once more in the background. Calls shrink to a fraction of their size with no visible loss: a three-minute excerpt went from 60 MB to 4 MB, a 9½-minute call from 68 MB to 17 MB. Text in shared screens stays as sharp as before.
- Compression only runs while nothing is being recorded or processed and the Mac is plugged in, and stops the moment that changes. Each result is checked frame by frame before it replaces the original. Turn it off in Settings › Recording, or run it on one recording with `anyrec compress`.
- The mixed audio is 96 kbps (was 192), which is plenty for speech.
