## Speakers

- **You are called by name.** Say who you are once in the Me row and transcripts, Markdown, subtitles, summaries and AI apps (MCP) use your name instead of "Me". New transcripts take it from your voice library.
- Lines nobody's voice claimed get their own row with a chooser, the one unnamed person from the call window offered first. In a two-person call with a readable call window, they go to the other person automatically.
- Parakeet now cuts a line where the other side starts or stops talking, so a quick reply with no pause or full stop is its own line and is credited to the right person.

## Transcript review

- The wording review now knows the call as a whole: its title, app, participants, your own vocabulary (Settings › Transcription) and the topic and terms used in it. A garbled spelling of a known term now gets corrected.

## Setup and engines

- Downloading the speaker models and Parakeet together in the welcome window could leave Parakeet unfinished. Fixed.
- An engine is only offered once it can actually run: Settings, the Engine menu and "Review with a second engine" no longer list engines without their model. A finished download switches its engine on, and the default goes Parakeet, then Whisper, then Apple.
- Microphone echo cancellation is now off by default, after a report that it lowered the call volume.

## Recording

- A Slack or Teams call that is going on is listed first in the menu bar panel, the File menu and the library's record button, with macOS's picker under Other Window….
- Picking a window from the library no longer pulls the window behind AnyRec to the front.

## Library and voices

- Importing voices: the open panel greyed out every `.anyrecvoices` file. Fixed, and `.json` files are accepted too. People are matched by name, nickname or call name.
- Slack screen-share tiles no longer produce names like "Costin's screen View Costin".
- In narrow windows the sidebar, then the inspector, step aside. The player controls and summary header wrap instead of squeezing their text. Resizing the window is smoother.
- New menu bar icons for each state, animated while background work runs.
