## Sharing with your team

- **One sync location.** Choose it from a recording's Sync button (**Choose Sync Location…**) the first time you sync. Your recordings go there, and meetings your colleagues sync there appear under **Shared with You**. Change or disconnect it in **Settings › Recording › Sharing**. Disconnecting no longer leaves the folder behind in the Sync button.
- **Shared meetings open in the same page as your own recordings**, read only: video, waveform, transcript, summary and the people in the call. Switch between the call audio and the microphone like on your own recordings. **Make a Copy** gives you a version you can edit.
- **Sync to more than one place.** **Sync to Another Location…** adds a copy in another folder, for example a client's. Each place has its own status and **Stop Syncing Here…**.
- **Deleting a synced recording** asks whether to keep the shared copies for your colleagues or remove them. Kept copies stay in the team folder, and you'll find them under **Shared with You**.
- Shared meetings say who shared them by the name on their Mac account.
- AnyRec waits for a shared recording to download from OneDrive or iCloud before playing it, and shows its progress. A recording its owner hasn't finished uploading says so.
- Meetings you sync no longer upload again every time AnyRec starts, and no longer leave conflict copies in the team folder.

When your colleagues update, their shared meetings get the new tracks, preview and sharer name automatically. The video isn't uploaded again.

## Library

- The sidebar is **All Recordings**, **Mine** and **Shared with You**, with **People** and **Unnamed Voices** under Speakers.
- A recording that still has people to name says so on its row ("1 person to name") instead of showing a small badge.

## Sound

- Calls recorded with headphones are no longer very quiet in the merged file. AnyRec used to turn the call down to match a quiet microphone; now it raises the quieter side instead, with a limiter. Your microphone's level is now measured only while the call could hear you. This applies to new recordings; ones merged before keep their sound.

## People in this call

- **Not sure who** now asks about leftover lines the call window showed someone talking on, one line at a time, with that person a click away.

## AI apps

- Claude Code, Claude Desktop and ChatGPT stay connected to AnyRec after your recordings moved to their new folder. They had been showing "Connection closed".
- The AnyRec server for AI apps no longer crashes when an app closes the connection.
