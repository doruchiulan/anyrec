## Fixed

- **Recording no longer crashes with high-sample-rate microphones.** A microphone running at 96 kHz (common for USB audio interfaces and some webcams) crashed AnyRec 0.8.4 the moment recording started. Rates above 48 kHz are now recorded at 44.1 or 48 kHz, and every track's settings are checked before writing begins, with a safe fallback.

## Downloads

- **AnyRec-0.8.5.zip**: Mac app for Apple Silicon and Intel.
- **anyrec-0.8.5-universal.tar.gz**: command-line tool with its embedded Whisper framework.
- **SHA256SUMS**: checksums of both downloads.

Both downloads are Developer ID signed and notarized. Requires macOS 15.2 or later.
