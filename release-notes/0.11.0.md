## Lines nobody claimed

- After the review, AnyRec now works out who said the short bits no voice could be matched to. They are mostly a word or two cut off someone's sentence, like "…we eliminate" / "them.". The review's model reads the conversation around each one and what the call window showed.
- Lines it's sure about are grouped with a single **Assign All**. Lines it thinks are likely are listed one by one, each with a short reason. Nothing is assigned until you say so, and one Undo takes back a whole batch.
- For recordings transcribed before this update, **Not sure who** has an **Ask Codex Who Said These** button (it names your review provider). Lines with a suggestion show the suggested name first, with **Review Suggestions** to jump to them.
- On a call with several people, the leftover bits are no longer offered to one person all at once. AnyRec offers to ask about the ones it hasn't asked about yet, or to leave them.

## People in this call

- **Who said each of these?** When AnyRec heard several people as one voice, and the call window showed different people talking, you now answer line by line. The name the window showed for each line is one click away.
- A small group of lines with less than 20 seconds of speech, or with nothing clean to play, is also asked about line by line instead of as one person.
- **Who is this?** now offers only people the call window actually showed talking.
- The question on screen stays put while you answer, instead of jumping to another person.

## Transcript

- The transcript in the inspector can show just one person's lines, or the lines nobody claimed. Pick from the filter menu at its top left.
- **Not sure who** lists its first five lines and opens the rest in the filtered transcript. Click a line's speaker label there to give it to someone, and it leaves the list.
- Lines nobody claimed now read **Not sure who** in the transcript, as they do in the People panel.

## Faster

- Answering a question in the People panel now takes a fraction of a second on long calls. On a three-hour Teams call it used to take several seconds.
- **Assign All** and **Fix All** apply a hundred lines in one go.

## Sync

- The sync button now shows clearly whether a meeting is synced: a filled blue cloud with a checkmark when it is, the plain outline Sync icon when it isn't. The cloud beside the recording's size matches the list's badge.

## Fixed

- The review's suggestions for unclaimed lines were listed twice.
