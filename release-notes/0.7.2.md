## Your voice is no longer turned down on calls

- Recording no longer lowers your microphone for the call. The echo cancellation added in 0.6.1 made macOS turn the microphone down 20 to 45 dB for every app using it, the call app included, so people on the call heard you much quieter while AnyRec was recording. Its own track also came out silent. It has been removed, with its Settings switch and the `--echo-cancellation` option.
- Echo on speaker calls is still handled the way it was before: the microphone is ducked under the other side in the mix, with the two tracks lined up first.
- Older recordings keep their `microphone-clean.m4a` on disk. Nothing reads it any more.
