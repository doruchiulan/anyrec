## Library

- Each recording's row now shows the date and time it started, and whose it is, the app and how long it ran, for example "Alex Morgan · Slack · 3:12". It no longer shows the first words of the summary.
- A small mark beside each row says whether it's yours or shared with you, matching **Mine** and **Shared with You** in the sidebar.
- The recording page and the inspector no longer show scroll bars.

## Downloads

- **AnyRec-0.12.1.dmg** contains the Mac app for Apple Silicon and Intel.
- **anyrec-0.12.1-universal.tar.gz** contains the command-line tool and its Whisper framework.
- **SHA256SUMS** lists the checksums for both downloads.

Requires macOS 15.2 or later.
