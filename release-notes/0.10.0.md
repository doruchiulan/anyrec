## People in this call

- The Speakers panel is replaced by **People in this call**. It asks one plain question at a time (Was this you? Who is this? Is this Madalin? Are these the same person?) and you answer by picking a person. Skip, back and Undo are always there.
- Below the questions are the people in the call. Open one to hear their samples. **Not Ciprian?** moves a whole person, or just one stray sample, to someone else.
- A voice that mixes two people can be sorted line by line. Bits nobody claimed are listed one by one, each with its own play button.
- Clicking a speaker's name in the transcript offers the people in this call first, then **Someone else…** with your whole voice library to search.

## A voice library you can trust

- AnyRec now checks before remembering a voice. It keeps the name for this call only, and says why, when the voice spoke for under 20 seconds, when the call window showed several people at once, or when it sounds more like someone else you've named.
- A sample that doesn't sound like the rest of a person's waits until a second one agrees, instead of matching them on every call.
- Each person keeps up to six of their best, most varied samples. Near-duplicates are skipped, and extras are set aside rather than deleted, so you can bring one back from People.
- People notices when two names are probably one person, and asks once: **Same Person** merges them and keeps the other name as a nickname.
- A voice that matched two people almost equally is no longer named after whichever came first.
- Fixed: your own name could be learned from a colleague in the same room, then credited to you on later calls. Names written as "Last, First (Contractor)" now match "First Last".

## Share and sync meetings

- **Share** sends a finished summary to a Slack channel or person through your Slack app, with the recording's link when there is one.
- **Sync** puts a meeting's playable recording, transcript and summary in a team folder that is already synced to Finder, such as a shared OneDrive or iCloud Drive folder. Your separate tracks and voice samples stay on your Mac.
- On another Mac, **Shared with me › Connect team folder…** lists the synced meetings. **Add to Library** follows your changes; **Make a Copy** makes an independent recording with its own names and summary.
- One cloud copy only: the sync menu says where a meeting stands (synced to which folder, up to date, uploading, or needs attention), and folders are named with the cloud that keeps them, like "AnyRec (iCloud Drive)". If someone edits or deletes the shared copy, AnyRec restores it from your original.
- Synced meetings show a cloud badge in the recordings list. The default sync folder is in **Settings › Recording › Sharing**.

## Library window

- The library window now uses a proper macOS toolbar, with a full-height inspector for the transcript, tracks and files.
- Resizing the window is smoother, with the video and captions kept live.

## Fixed

- **AnyRec no longer freezes during a long transcription.** Background work was running at the same priority as the windows, which froze the app for seconds on Apple silicon and for the whole transcription on Intel Macs. It now runs in the background where it belongs, and Intel Macs transcribe one piece at a time.
