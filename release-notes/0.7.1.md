## The app updates itself

- AnyRec now checks for and installs updates itself (Sparkle). Automatic checks start only once you agree (asked on the second launch, or in Settings › General). **Check for Updates…** is in the AnyRec menu.
- An update never interrupts a call: no update window appears while recording, during a call or while a recording is processed (the menu bar panel shows it instead), and the relaunch waits until nothing is being recorded or saved.
- Every update is signed, and the app checks the signature before installing it.

## After a recording

- The panel after a recording shows a checklist of what happens next: saved, prepared, transcribed and summarised. Level warnings that nothing could be done about are gone. **New Recording** replaces Done, since processing carries on in the background.

## Homebrew

- The `anyrec` command installed with Homebrew had not started since 0.5.0 (macOS refused its Whisper framework after Homebrew rewrote it). It is re-signed on install now.
