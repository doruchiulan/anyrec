## Choosing a speaker

- Choose **Someone else…** wherever you assign a speaker, including beside **Fix** on a suggested line. Search for someone from this call or an earlier call, or enter a new name.
- If you pick a different speaker for one line, **Fix All** keeps your choice and applies the remaining suggestions.

## Unnamed Voices

- The library's **Unnamed Voices** filter now includes recordings with lines marked **Not sure who**. A recording leaves the filter once every line has a named speaker, even if an old unnamed voice still has an empty entry.
