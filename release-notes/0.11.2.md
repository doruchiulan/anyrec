## Mac installer

Open the AnyRec disk image and drag the app into Applications. The app has a new icon that matches the AnyRec website. If you already have AnyRec, you can update from inside the app.

## Downloads

- **AnyRec-0.11.2.dmg** contains the Mac app for Apple Silicon and Intel.
- **anyrec-0.11.2-universal.tar.gz** contains the command-line tool and its Whisper framework.
- **SHA256SUMS** lists the checksums for both downloads.

Requires macOS 15.2 or later.
